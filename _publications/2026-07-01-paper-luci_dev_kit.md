This paper introduces the software platform underlying our powered wheelchair research (see [A Minimal, Deterministic Approach to Shared Control for Safe Powered Wheelchair Driving](/publication/rss2026shared_control_wheelchair)): a ROS 2 (Python/C++) codebase for interfacing with a commercial powered wheelchair equipped with LUCI assistive drive technology, covering human input handling, shared control, command arbitration, sensor stream processing, odometry, and navigation via the ROS Nav Stack.

*Equal contribution.
